# Excavator additive sibling proposal reuse

The experimental scripts have been removed from the active project. Command examples below are historical records, not supported commands. This archive retains reports and JSON evidence; local `.stacks/benchmarks/` inputs and native builds are not included.

Adding refreshed sibling proposals alongside the complete fresh CEM pool improved physically successful transition throughput in all four paired trials. Raw new-node throughput was essentially flat at 16 stones and improved modestly at 26 stones. Selected actions and immediate rewards were unchanged. These results support further investigation, not a production performance claim.

Follow-up: the [broader-bank audit](excavator-broader-additive-proposal-reuse.md) found no additional short-only root proposals in four fresh predecessor searches. With the current upfront root long-validation policy, broadening eligibility leaves the bank unchanged.

The subsequent [combined sibling/descendant comparison](excavator-combined-proposal-reuse.md) found additional viable proposals at 16 stones, but adding them lowered pooled nodes/s by 7.0% relative to the existing sibling bank. At 26 stones, no descendants were added. The combined policy has since been retired; its report is retained for reference.

## Method

Eight native CUDA searches ran on 2026-10-07: two scenes, two next-search seeds (0 and 1), and baseline/additive variants. Each uses the current excavator CUDA profile, 1,024 iterations, maximum depth 4 and required descendant validation. All complete without timeouts. The Ryzen 9 9900X / RTX 5060 Ti trials run sequentially, with BLAS/OpenMP threads limited to one. Variant order is reversed between seeds within each scene.

The same saved tree artifacts used in the earlier CUDA experiments supply the selected subtree and sibling bank. The trees were generated by CUDA predecessor searches with seed 0. After committing the selected placement, the measured scenes contain 16 and 26 stones, with 49 total inventory objects. The bank contains two and three original-scene long-valid sibling proposals, respectively. Both variants preserve and revalidate the same subtree and invoke the corrected production promotion lifecycle.

The additive policy was implemented only in the now-removed experimental script `tests/benchmark_excavator_proposal_reuse.py`:

1. Run normal fresh generation and root admission. Wait until the current root pool is exhausted or the normal root allocation is full after validation. In all measured additive runs, CEM has returned all 32 fresh candidates before bank admission.
2. Re-solve bank proposals against the new scene from their previous solved poses, refresh scores and support metadata, and apply current filters and duplicate checks against retained root candidates. No old evaluated states, forces, rewards or backup statistics are transferred.
3. Append eligible proposals in a separate allowance of at most eight. The existing two-per-object CEM rule also caps this bank independently; fresh CEM still applies its own two-per-object limit. Consequently, the combined root can contain more variants of an object than fresh CEM alone. Fresh candidates are never removed to make space.
4. Keep the fresh-generation cursor unchanged. Use the normal authoritative long-validation routine for the added roots and normal MCTS backup for passing results. Descendant requirements are unchanged. Increase the final-selection candidate allowance from 32 to 32 plus the number admitted so extras cannot crowd candidates out through that cap.

All fresh candidate poses, identities, return counts and pagination offsets match the paired baseline exactly, verified by hashes. Normal fresh generation remains fully funded. The experiment tests additional exploration, not savings from replacing fresh generation. It does not broaden the bank to short-only candidates, and does not test repeated reuse over multiple future placements.

No root retry or floor fallback occurs in these eight measured next searches. The prototype admits the bank only once per search; fresh-budget behavior across subsequent retry pools requires a separate test before production use.

## Admission

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 16 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 33 | 0.074 s |
| 26 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 35 | 0.129–0.131 s |

At 16 stones, one of the two proposals is excluded by the bank duplicate/limit check. At 26 stones all three are admitted, then one fails long validation. Refresh/admission time excludes their long validation and subsequent exploration; all of those costs are included in total search time.

| Stones | Baseline nodes/s | Additive nodes/s | Change | Baseline physical passes/s | Additive physical passes/s | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 16 | 14.356 | 14.293 | -0.4% | 1.911 | 2.122 | +11.1% |
| 26 | 5.764 | 5.939 | +3.0% | 0.429 | 0.501 | +17.0% |

At 16 stones, the raw-rate effect reverses between seeds: +4.9% and -5.2%. Physical-pass rates improve in both (+12.5% and +10.0%). At 26 stones, raw rates improve +0.5% and +9.2%, and physical-pass rates improve +7.3% and +35.9%. The small raw differences should not be treated as established speedups from two fixed predecessor histories.

New nodes means generated child nodes, including failures and the admitted bank nodes, excluding inherited nodes. It is not the count of parent-expansion operations or only feasible states. Physical passes deduplicate new transitions by exact parent scene and action, exclude inherited transitions, prioritize long results over short results, and use the latest result at equal fidelity. Some passes have only short-fidelity evidence; this metric is not a count of executable actions or validated descendant paths.

The search timer includes bank refresh, validation, exploration and final selection, but excludes input loading, environment setup, restored-observation reconstruction and output serialization.

## Selection quality

Both variants select object 51 with reward 4.176 at 16 stones, and object 53 with reward 1.741 at 26 stones. No selected action comes directly from the bank. All selections have a validated descendant path.

| Stones | Seed | Baseline selected validated path return | Additive selected validated path return |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 16 | 0 | 13.9524 | 13.6236 |
| 16 | 1 | 13.5128 | 13.2316 |
| 26 | 0 | 13.9334 | 13.9334 |
| 26 | 1 | 13.9334 | 13.9334 |

More physically successful transitions did not improve the selected placement or validated return. The result is nevertheless less adverse than the earlier reserved-slot policy at 26 stones: additive admission avoids displacing fresh alternatives. Keep it experimental. More predecessor histories are needed to establish performance; broadening to short-passing proposals is a separate experiment, and prior long failures must not be silently treated as valid short-only proposals.

## Validation and reproduction

Eight targeted session/admission tests passed. The additive check uses actual search orchestration to verify that two normal roots plus one added root all survive admission, the fresh cursor remains two, all three receive long evaluation, and final selection can consider the extra root. Native output checks verify identical paired input hashes, profile snapshots, fresh action hashes, preserved-edge counts and revalidation outcomes, complete iteration budgets, and unchanged source/profile/native-module hashes during measurement.

```bash
export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1
export QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen MPLCONFIGDIR=/tmp/stacking-mpl
python tests/benchmark_excavator_proposal_reuse.py baseline \
  --config apps/excavator/config/mcts_cuda.yml --seed 0 \
  --input .stacks/benchmarks/proposal-reuse-20261007/15.pkl \
  --output .stacks/benchmarks/additive-repeat/16-baseline-0.json
python tests/benchmark_excavator_proposal_reuse.py additive_reuse \
  --config apps/excavator/config/mcts_cuda.yml --seed 0 \
  --input .stacks/benchmarks/proposal-reuse-20261007/15.pkl \
  --output .stacks/benchmarks/additive-repeat/16-additive-0.json
```

Use `25.pkl` for the 26-stone scene and repeat with `--seed 1`. Inputs must be trusted local pickles; existing outputs are not overwritten. See the [earlier experiment](excavator-proposal-reuse.md) for preparation commands.
